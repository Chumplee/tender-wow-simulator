# TENDER: A World of Warcraft Life Simulator

A real Godot 4.7 project. He sits at his PC playing WoW. Forever.
There is no win condition. There is no logout button.

## The start screen

1985 arcade style, just like his: blinking INSERT COIN, a HI-SCORES table
(Tender 999999, Chumplee 025000, Hana 000404, Sleep 000000), PRESS START,
and a chiptune that starts when you press it (square-wave, composed in code).

## The room

Modeled on the reference Mochi provided: sage-green walls, the big window
with the city skyline and palm silhouettes he never looks at, the couch
labeled BED (UNUSED) with abandoned clothes on it, a messy coffee table,
the glowing retired PC tower, Dew cans and discs everywhere.

Tender himself is drawn from the photo Mochi supplied: the glorious halo
of dark curls, the long beard (now with emergency crumb storage), wide
raider eyes, wrist brace on the mouse hand, typing forever.

## Things to click

- **KILL WI-FI** (or click the router): DISCONNECTED screen, red face,
  steam, screaming about his ping. The router reconnects itself after a
  while and he takes full credit.
- **YANK PLUG** (or click the power strip): black monitor, a gasp, then a
  full sobbing breakdown with streaming tears until you plug him back in.
- **FEED SNACK**: the can pyramid grows, crumbs go in the beard.
- **CURTAINS** (or click the window): sunlight. He hisses and closes the
  blinds himself at record speed.
- **BED (UNUSED)**: click the couch to suggest bed. He will explain why
  sleep is a debuff.
- **POKE**: he has thoughts about being poked by the cursor of god.
- The monitor, his face, and the snack reserves are all clickable too.

Days roll past the window (a full day/night cycle every 36 seconds), the
/played counter climbs past 19,432 hours, and every snack and every day
adds to the trash pile in the corner. A witness log keeps the receipts and
a fake patch-notes ticker scrolls along the bottom.

## Run / export

- Open the folder in Godot 4.7.2 and press Play, or
- play the exported web build in `build/web/` (serve the folder over
  http, e.g. upload it to GitHub Pages or itch.io; thread support is off
  in the export preset so it works on plain static hosting).
